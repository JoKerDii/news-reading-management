## Where AI is creating real value in real estate
source: McKinsey & Company
date: May 2026
url: https://www.mckinsey.com/featured-insights/mckinsey-explainers/where-ai-is-creating-real-value-in-real-estate
tag: Report

McKinsey argues real estate is moving past isolated AI pilots toward end-to-end, agentic workflow transformation across leasing, underwriting, asset management, and operations. It maps where AI is generating measurable value today versus where adoption remains experimental, concluding the technology is beginning to redefine roles and reshape how property organizations operate rather than simply automating discrete tasks.

## Procurement in Financial Services: A Tech-Powered Value Creator
source: Stefano Sironi et al · Bain & Company
date: 2026-05-28
url: https://www.bain.com/insights/procurement-in-financial-services-a-tech-powered-value-creator/
tag: Report

Bain finds that rising spend on cloud, analytics, cybersecurity, and generative AI is elevating procurement at banks and insurers from a back-office cost center into a strategic, AI-enabled value driver. It sets out three priorities for procurement leaders: unlocking savings, transforming operating models, and building next-generation AI-powered procurement hubs. Firms adopting more advanced operating models have achieved staff-efficiency gains above 30% (some reaching 50%) while saving 5%–8% on addressable third-party spend.

## Middle East Economy Watch — May 2026: GCC's roadmap for resilience
source: Richard Boxshall & Jing Teow · PwC Middle East
date: 2026-05-20
url: https://www.pwc.com/m1/en/publications/middle-east-economy-watch/may-2026.html
tag: Report

PwC examines how the regional conflict and near-halt of traffic through the Strait of Hormuz triggered a severe energy and supply-chain shock for GCC economies, citing roughly a 40% drop in crude output across Strait-bordering producers and a contraction in Saudi Q1 2026 oil activity. It documents the adaptive response—rerouting cargo overland and via Red Sea ports, central-bank liquidity measures, currency swaps, and fiscal stimulus—and argues the longer-term priority is resilience through trade-route diversification, hardened infrastructure, and rail corridors such as the Gulf Railway.

## EY Global IPO Trends Q1 2026
source: EY Global
date: May 2026
url: https://www.ey.com/en_gl/insights/ipo/trends
tag: Report

EY reports global IPO markets entered 2026 with cautious optimism, supported by market stability, the prospect of lower capital costs, and several large, well-prepared issuers pricing and trading well early in the quarter. Renewed tariff uncertainty, private-credit concerns, a software-sector selloff, and Middle East conflict drove volatility, leading some deals to be postponed. Defense produced the largest global IPO of the quarter, and investor demand concentrated in large, scaled companies across aerospace, defense, and AI infrastructure.

## Digital Rails, Real Economics: Digital Assets and the Future of Wholesale Banking
source: Dylan Walsh, Jason Ekberg, Joseph Cox, Jordan Cole et al · Oliver Wyman (with Morgan Stanley)
date: 2026-05-14
url: https://www.oliverwyman.com/our-expertise/insights/2026/may/digital-assets-future-wholesale-banking.html
tag: Report

Oliver Wyman's annual wholesale banking outlook estimates the industry generated record revenues of $660 billion in 2025 and projects $650–850 billion in revenue with a 12%–20% return on equity by 2030. It concludes digital assets are unlikely to become a trading asset class rivaling existing businesses or to disrupt deposit funding, but the migration of payments, collateral, liquidity, and servicing onto "digital rails" could reshape over $200 billion in revenues and place $46 billion at risk. Early movers that embed digital assets into core workflows are best positioned to defend market share.

## The Global Cities Driving the Next Era of Business Growth (Cities Shaping the Future 2026)
source: Ben Simpfendorfer & Matthew Tucker · Oliver Wyman Forum
date: 2026-05-19
url: https://www.oliverwymanforum.com/performance-reimagined/cities-shaping-future-business-growth-and-innovation.html
tag: Report

The Oliver Wyman Forum ranks 1,500 cities—accounting for over 75% of global GDP ($88 trillion) and 92% of listed companies—on five factors: commercial vibrancy, connectivity, supply chains, innovation, and climate resilience. It finds globalization is evolving rather than retreating, with midsize and emerging-market cities (from Ho Chi Minh City and Tangier to Austin and Hefei) gaining on traditional hubs through affordability and supply-chain pivots. The report urges CEOs to diversify operations and pursue acquisitions, and city leaders to compete on visa policy, talent, and climate resilience.

## Gartner Forecasts Worldwide AI Spending to Grow 47% in 2026
source: Gartner
date: 2026-05-19
url: https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2026-05-19-gartner-forecasts-worldwide-ai-spending-to-grow-47-percent-in-2026
tag: Report

Gartner forecasts worldwide AI spending will reach $2.59 trillion in 2026, a 47% year-over-year increase. AI infrastructure—AI-optimized IaaS, servers, network fabric, and semiconductors—will be the largest segment at over 45% of spending, with AI-optimized server spend expected to triple over five years. Gartner also projects AI agent software spending of $206.5 billion in 2026, rising to $376.3 billion in 2027.

## Gartner Says Autonomous Business and AI Layoffs May Create Budget Room, but Do Not Deliver Returns
source: Gartner
date: 2026-05-05
url: https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2026-05-05-gartner-says-autonomous-business-and-artificial-intelligence-layoffs-may-create-budget-room-but-do-not-deliver-returns
tag: Report

Gartner cautions that 2026 is an inflection year for enterprise AI spending, but organizations show limited appetite for using AI to drive disruptive change, favoring tactical initiatives with incremental efficiency gains. It warns that AI-driven layoffs and "autonomous business" cost cuts may free up budget without delivering durable returns, urging leaders to tie AI investment to measurable value rather than headcount reduction.

## Global Real Estate Perspective — May 2026
source: JLL Research
date: May 2026
url: https://www.jll.com/en-us/insights/market-perspectives/global
tag: Report

JLL reports global direct real estate transaction volumes of $216 billion in Q1 2026, up 18% year over year, with Asia Pacific the strongest region (+31%), led by Japan and a record quarter in Singapore. U.S. investment activity expanded, with San Francisco up 150% year over year, followed by Chicago, Atlanta, Washington D.C., and New York. Office, industrial/logistics, and retail posted the highest year-over-year growth, supported by robust debt-market liquidity and stable pricing.

## 2026 Global Allergen Specific Immunotherapy Development Blue Book
source: Frost & Sullivan
date: 2026-05-11
url: https://www.newsfilecorp.com/release/296871/Frost-Sullivan-Released-Report-2026-Global-Allergen-Specific-Immunotherapy-Development-Blue-Book
tag: Report

Frost & Sullivan analyzes the global allergen-specific immunotherapy (AIT) sector, framing AIT as the only etiological treatment able to alter the natural history of allergic disease as it shifts from "empirical treatment" to "precision immunomodulation." It traces four generations of technology from crude natural extracts to recombinant allergens, reviews delivery routes (SCIT, SLIT, microneedles, oral films), and highlights unmet needs—poor adherence, lack of standardization, and low awareness, with fewer than 10% of China's 100M+ allergy patients aware of AIT, marking a major growth opportunity.
