## Magnanimity

tag: X

Galloway argues for U.S. magnanimity toward Cuba as a strategic alternative to military pressure and regime-change ultimatums, grounding the case in America's post-WWII Marshall Plan and Japan rebuilding as proof that soft power — unconditional generosity — has historically delivered greater geopolitical returns than force.
