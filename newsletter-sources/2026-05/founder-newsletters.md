## The internet is being rebuilt for machines
source: Rebecca Bellan · TechCrunch
date: 2026-05-28
url: https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/28/the-internet-is-being-rebuilt-for-machines/
tag: Agents

Cloud infrastructure built for predictable human traffic is being redesigned for AI agents that spike and vanish unpredictably. AWS launched a new OpenSearch Serverless that decouples compute from storage and scales to zero when idle. With Cloudflare reporting bots at 31% of HTTP traffic and non-human traffic projected to exceed human traffic by 2027, Databricks, Snowflake, Microsoft, and Cloudflare are all repositioning around agentic workloads.
