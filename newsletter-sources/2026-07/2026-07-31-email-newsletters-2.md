### 19. [The Reverse Information Paradox](https://x.com/satyanadella/status/2076323181154230284)

**Published:** 2026-07-12 | **By:** Satya Nadella · X

**Summary:** Nadella inverts Kenneth Arrow's information paradox, in which a seller risks giving away knowledge to sell it. He argues that with AI the buyer gives away knowledge to use what they bought, paying once in money and again in proprietary context revealed through prompts, tool use, corrections, and evals. **Published:** 2026-07-22 | **By:** Christine Zhu · X

**Summary:** Zhu argues that because models are grown rather than designed, managing AI products resembles gardening: creating conditions for growth rather than specifying outputs, making systems thinking table stakes for PMs. Drawing on Donella Meadows, she notes that system behaviour arises from how components connect, so optimizing individual domains produces a broken whole, especially since agents act across a product without regard for the org chart. **Published:** 2026-07-26 | **By:** Shreyas Doshi · Shreyas Free Newsletter

### 47. [Big-World Intuitions](https://sarahconstantin.substack.com/p/big-world-intuitions)

**Published:** 2026-07-30 | **By:** Sarah Constantin · Rough Diamonds

**Summary:** Describes big-world heuristics: strategies that work when an actor is small relative to its environment, such as a startup in a large market, a small trader, an r-selected species, or a researcher far from solving a problem.
