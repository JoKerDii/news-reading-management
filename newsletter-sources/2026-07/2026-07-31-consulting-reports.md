## Boston Consulting Group (BCG)

### 3. [BCG's Intelligent Cities Index 2026: How AI Shapes Urban Living (Index)](https://www.bcg.com/publications/2026/how-ai-is-shaping-intelligent-cities)

**Published:** 2026-07-27 | **By:** Boston Consulting Group

**Summary:** BCG's inaugural Intelligent Cities Index assesses how 61 of the world's largest cities across 39 countries use technology to serve residents and businesses. London ranks first, followed by Dubai, New York City, Washington DC and Amsterdam. Cities are scored across five domains: outcomes, strategy, adoption, ways of working, and enablers. **Published:** 2026-07-21 | **By:** Boston Consulting Group

**Summary:** BCG's 2026 Digital Government Citizen Survey covers 48,500 respondents in 44 countries, its largest to date. It reports that 64% of respondents globally use AI at least weekly, while satisfaction with government digital services has fallen 13 percentage points over the past decade. ## Bain & Company

**Published:** 2026-07-16 | **By:** Kurt Grichel · Bain & Company (with NielsenIQ)

**Summary:** Bain's analysis of NielsenIQ data finds US grocery unit sales down roughly 2% year on year in most months since February, and down 1.8% in June, while grocery prices continue rising 2% to 3%. Bain attributes the contraction to cumulative inflation, a 20% March rise in gas prices, reduced SNAP participation, online basket sizes and GLP-1 adoption. ## Oliver Wyman

### 10. [The Industrial AI Divide: How AI Leaders Are Pulling Ahead Across Transportation, Logistics, and Defense (Industry Report)](https://www.oliverwymanforum.com/mobility/ai-divide-transportation-logistics-defense.html)

**Published:** 2026-07-14 | **By:** Oliver Wyman Forum (with UC Berkeley)

**Summary:** Drawing on interviews with more than 50 senior executives plus executive surveys and market analysis, the report finds only 8% of transportation, logistics and defense CEOs say their organizations use AI at scale. Early adopters report measurable gains in safety, performance and cost, with AI-based rail coordination cutting fuel use by up to 45% and improving travel times by more than 30%. The authors argue the largest gains come from orchestrating systems rather than automating individual tasks.

## Roland Berger

### 11. [AI in customer service: from hype to measurable results (Study)](https://www.rolandberger.com/en/Insights/Publications/AI-in-customer-service-from-hype-to-measurable-value.html)

**Published:** 2026-07-07 | **By:** Simone Schatto, Steffen Thiel, Fabian Huhle and Maryna Finkelshteyn · Roland Berger

**Summary:** Based on a survey with Potloc of more than 550 senior decision-makers across five industries and three regions, the study finds reported AI use in customer service fell from 95% to 54% year on year, reflecting a narrower definition of what counts as AI deployment. Over half of adopters report significant improvements in response times, satisfaction and operating cost. Legacy systems, data quality and unclear governance remain the main barriers to scaling.
