### 2. [Aftershocks: Energy Security Beyond the Strait of Hormuz Crisis (McKinsey Global Institute research)](https://www.mckinsey.com/mgi/our-research/aftershocks-energy-security-beyond-the-strait-of-hormuz-crisis)

**Published:** 2026-09-17 | **By:** Tiago Devesa, Mekala Krishnan, Humayun Tai, Chris Bradley, Shubham Singhal, and colleagues · McKinsey Global Institute

**Summary:** The 2026 Hormuz disruption affected about one-fifth of global oil and gas supply. It showed resilience through inventories and bypass pipelines, but also that the system's shock absorbers are thinning. Measures underway could offset 35–70 percent of pre-crisis flows by 2030, though delivery is uncertain. The report argues energy security means managing dependencies across time horizons rather than eliminating them.

### 3. [The Key to AI Value Is Hiding in Plain Sight: Your Operating Model (survey-based article)](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/people-and-organization/our-insights/the-key-to-ai-value-is-hiding-in-plain-sight-your-operating-model)

**Published:** 2026-09-09 | **By:** Brooke Weddle, Deepak Mahadevan, Richard Steele, Tom Welchman · McKinsey

**Summary:** Organizations succeeding with AI, which McKinsey calls "reinventors", do not share a single best operating model. They make deliberate choices across 12 interconnected elements, organize around end-to-end value creation, and use adaptive governance with faster resource reallocation. Nearly half of reinventors capture meaningful enterprise value from AI, versus 13% of organizations in earlier adoption phases.

## Boston Consulting Group (BCG)

### 4. [In Search of a Wider Recovery: The 2026 M&A Report](https://www.bcg.com/publications/2026/m-and-a-rebounds-recovery-uneven)

**Published:** 2026-09-21 | **By:** Jens Kengelbach, Daniel Friedman, Dominik Degen, Christoph Schweizer · BCG

**Summary:** Global M&A value rose 15% year over year through August 2026, but the recovery is concentrated in large deals while smaller transactions lag. The main constraint has shifted from capital availability to execution, particularly asset readiness and valuation alignment. A broader recovery depends on more portfolio rotation, transaction-ready assets, and flexible deal structures.

### 5. [The Three Dimensions of Leadership for the AI Era (survey of 563 executives)](https://www.bcg.com/publications/2026/three-leadership-roles-ceos-ai)

**Published:** 2026-09-29 | **By:** Brittany Heflin, Gabriella Kellerman, Robert Werner, David Martin, Julia Dhar, Vladimir Lukic · BCG

**Summary:** Drawing on a survey of 563 executives, BCG finds that leaders who combine three roles (visionary, codeveloper, and steward) outperform. Those who excel at all three and pair that with advanced AI maturity see about an 80% performance lift. Organizations with people-centered transformations are three times more likely to reach AI maturity.

## Bain & Company

### 6. [New Innovation Is Required to Fund AI's $6 Trillion Buildout (Technology Report 2026)](https://www.bain.com/insights/new-innovation-is-required-to-fund-ais-6-trillion-buildout-technology-report-2026/)

**Published:** 2026-09-29 | **By:** David Crawford, Kristie Tagawa, Cory Boles, Tatum Quinn · Bain & Company

## CBRE

### 16. [AI's Impact on Office Demand (research report)](https://www.cbre.com/insights/reports/ais-impact-on-office-demand)

**Published:** 2026-09-09 | **By:** Henry Chin, Julie Whelan, Stefan Weiss, Dennis Schoenmaker, Christina Tong · CBRE Research

**Summary:** CBRE finds only 5% of office workers face high AI displacement risk, while 18% are highly adaptive. Roles most exposed to automation skew remote, while AI-supporting roles favor in-person work. CBRE forecasts US office vacancy falling to 14.5% by 2031, with San Francisco, Washington DC, and Boston best placed to benefit.
