## McKinsey & Company

### 1. [The State of AI in 2026: On the Road to ROI (Global Survey)](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/quantumblack/our-insights/the-state-of-ai)

**Published:** 2026-08-25 | **By:** Dan Tinkoff, Lieven Van der Veken, Michael Chui, Tara Balakrishnan · McKinsey & Company

**Summary:** McKinsey's annual global AI survey (1,719 respondents in 97 countries) finds 44% of organizations now scaling AI enterprise-wide, with large enterprises scaling agents at 40%, up from 27%. Yet enterprise financial impact is flat: 37% attribute any EBIT gain to AI, unchanged from 2025, even as 80% of individuals report productivity gains. Notably, 32% of organizations skipped buying software because agentic coding tools let them build in-house, and 39% expect AI-related headcount declines next year.

## BCG

### 2. [Global Consumers Have Moved On. Has Your Growth Strategy Caught Up? (Global Consumer Survey)](https://www.bcg.com/publications/2026/five-consumer-shifts-reshaping-growth)

**Published:** 2026-08-24 | **By:** Tony Portera and colleagues · Boston Consulting Group

**Summary:** Based on a survey of more than 13,000 consumers across 12 markets and analysis of over 1,000 brands, BCG identifies five durable themes reshaping demand: value over price, the longevity mindset, solo living, a new trust compass, and AI-guided decision making. Nearly 70% of consumers will not buy a product they can afford if it lacks perceived value. BCG argues companies that adapt value propositions and prepare for AI-guided purchasing can capture outsize growth.

## Bain & Company

### 3. [Gaming Report 2026 (Annual Industry Report)](https://www.bain.com/insights/topics/gaming-report/)

**Published:** 2026-08-18 | **By:** Anders Christofferson, Anders Videbaek, Daniel Hong, and colleagues · Bain & Company

**Summary:** Drawing on a survey of 5,300+ gamers and analysis of 100 titles released since 2023, Bain's third annual gaming report argues the "average gamer" no longer exists: the top 20% of spenders drive roughly 75% of spending and the most active 20% account for nearly 60% of playtime. Games built for a specific target player succeeded 83% of the time versus 50% otherwise. Nearly half of gamers now buy directly from developer web stores, and personalized offers move purchases far more than blanket discounts.

## Gallup

### 10. [AI's Effect on Workplace Culture (Survey Analysis)](https://www.gallup.com/workplace/712976/ai-effect-workplace-culture.aspx)

**Published:** 2026-08-16 | **By:** Morgan Meinen and Megan Mulherin · Gallup

**Summary:** Combining Gallup's Q1 2026 workforce study of 23,717 US employees with a survey of 102 CHROs, the analysis finds AI adoption pushing workplace culture in both directions: in AI-adopting organizations, 24% of employees say culture improved over the past year while 25% say it worsened. Managers are the deciding factor — employees whose managers actively support AI use are far likelier to report improved culture (31% vs. 21%). Half of CHROs lack confidence in managers' ability to guide employees' AI use, and 57% now provide manager AI training.
