### 5. [How to Invest in IPOs](https://www.appeconomyinsights.com/p/how-to-invest-in-new-ipos)

**Published:** 2026-06-19 | **By:** Bertrand Seguin · App Economy Insights

**Summary:** Drawing on Jay Ritter's 40-year dataset, the article argues most IPOs underperform: half post negative five-year returns from the first-day close and only 24% become multi-baggers, with the worst damage clustering 6–24 months out as lock-ups expire. The first-day pop typically benefits allocated investors, not open-market buyers. The recommended playbook is to avoid day-one hype, wait for the second earnings call, size positions small, and anchor to valuation, treating SpaceX, OpenAI, and Anthropic as watch-list names.
