## Bain & Company

### 1. [e-Conomy SEA 2026 (Annual Digital Economy Report)](https://www.bain.com/insights/e-conomy-sea-2026/)

**Published:** 2026-10-21 | **By:** Florian Hoppe, Willy Chang, Aadarsh Baijal · Bain & Company, with Temasek and Google

**Summary:** Generative AI has reached about 243 million people in Southeast Asia's six largest markets, or 58% of the working population, in under three years, though per-capita use is roughly a tenth of US levels. Only about 1-2% of the population pays for generative AI, while 69% of consumers use AI to compare products. The region's digital economy is on track for 17% GMV growth toward $1 trillion, and AI usage could rise up to 60 times today's level by 2030.

## Deloitte

### 1. [The State of AI in the Enterprise: Built Differently (Global Survey)](https://www.deloitte.com/us/en/about/press-room/enterprise-ai-enters-an-roi-reckoning.html)

**Published:** 2026-10-29 | **By:** Deloitte

**Summary:** Most surveyed companies report faster decisions and execution from AI, and 28% now report revenue gains, up from 20% in January, though only 25% use AI to fundamentally transform their business. Organizational change is the main obstacle to value: only 15% report a significant shift in decision-making authority after redesigning work. Forty percent of enterprises allow AI to take some actions without human approval, yet 23% say accountability is unclear when an AI system causes a negative outcome.

## PwC

### 1. [2027 Global Digital Trust Insights (Global Survey)](https://www.pwc.com/gx/en/news-room/press-releases/2026/senior-leaders-cyber-budget-rise.html)

**Published:** 2026-10-01 | **By:** PwC

**Summary:** Among nearly 4,000 business and technology executives in 71 countries, 84% of security and finance leaders expect cyber budgets to grow, and 58% of security leaders rank AI among their top budget priorities. Only 39% have a fully operational continuity plan that addresses cyber risks, and half name attacks on AI systems as a threat they are least ready to handle. Fewer than half strongly agree that cyber risk is a standing board agenda item, and only 22% would let autonomous AI agents run cyber defence without human sign-off.

### 2. [Emerging Trends in Real Estate 2027 — Canada (Industry Outlook)](https://www.pwc.com/ca/en/media/release/pwc-2027-emerging-trends-in-real-estate-report.html)

**Published:** 2026-10-27 | **By:** PwC Canada with the Urban Land Institute

**Summary:** Investor sentiment in Canadian real estate is at its highest in 15 years, but the recovery is uneven across property types, regions, and sources of capital. Senior housing, retail, and industrial properties are the leading bets for 2027, while data centres and care facilities are emerging at the intersection of real estate and infrastructure. Debt is widely available, but equity is the main constraint ahead of a 2027 refinancing wave, and structured capital is filling the gap.

## EY

### 1. [AI Drives Productivity, but CEOs Struggle to Turn Gains into Growth (EY-Parthenon CEO Outlook Survey)](https://www.ey.com/en_gl/newsroom/2026/10/ai-drives-productivity-but-ceos-struggle-to-turn-gains-into-grow)

**Published:** October 2026 | **By:** EY-Parthenon

**Summary:** Half of the 1,200 surveyed CEOs credit AI as the top driver of recent productivity gains, ahead of business process redesign. Only 16% have real-time visibility into AI returns, and 23% say AI-driven gains are often absorbed before they reach the bottom line. Skills gaps are the bigger constraint, with 72% expecting talent shortages to limit growth more than access to capital within three years.

## Oliver Wyman

### 1. [The Reality Check: Beyond the AI Hype, Hope, and Hysteria (Oliver Wyman Forum Report)](https://www.oliverwyman.com/media-center/2026/oct/rapid-ai-capability-development-is-creating-opportunities-for-those-companies-with-leadership-that-translate-productivity-gains-into-business-value.html)

**Published:** 2026-10-15 | **By:** Oliver Wyman Forum

**Summary:** Only 8% of workplace AI users say their organization has a company-wide standard for measuring time saved by AI. CEOs reporting AI returns above 10% were 4.4 times as likely to have deployed AI at scale in new products, services, and revenue streams (40% versus 9%). Also, 88% of users report productivity gains when approved AI is accessible and clearly governed, versus 52% where it is not, and overly strict rules push 63% of employees toward unapproved tools.

## Korn Ferry

### 1. [2027 Talent Trends Report (Workforce Survey)](https://www.kornferry.com/about-us/press/korn-ferry-report-ai-and-capability-gaps-will-reshape-talent-in-2027)

**Published:** 2026-10-22 | **By:** Korn Ferry

**Summary:** Leaders see capability gaps as a bigger challenge than talent shortages (77%), and 78% say too few people are ready for how work will change, with adaptability ranked the top workforce skill for 2027. The report outlines seven shifts, including AI agents taking over routine tasks (63%), work organized around skills (65%), and employees owning their development (91%). Sixty-nine percent expect tighter collaboration among hiring, learning and development, and workforce planning teams.
